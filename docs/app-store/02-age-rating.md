# Age Rating Questionnaire — Stick Picks

App Store Connect asks 19 questions about content. These are our
answers + rationale. Final computed rating: **17+**.

## Why 17+

Any app that has tobacco references "of a frequent or intense nature"
gets 17+ by Apple's rules. Stick Picks is built around cigar
collecting, so even framed as a collection/journal tool, tobacco
references are central. 17+ is the correct, defensible answer.

We additionally enforce **21+ inside the app** via a mandatory age
gate — that's stricter than the App Store rating, which is fine.
App Store rating is the maximum allowed by Apple's taxonomy; our
in-app gate sits on top of it.

## Anti-pattern to avoid

Do NOT answer "Infrequent/Mild" on #8 hoping to get a lower rating.
Apple will catch this during review (the app's entire catalog is
tobacco products) and the app will be rejected for misrepresentation.
**Frequent/Intense is the honest answer.** 17+ is not bad for a
cigar collection app — it matches the user's legal-age expectation.

---

## Age-rating refresh on updates

If a future version changes the content shape (e.g., you add a social
feed with user-generated content), re-run this questionnaire. For
**collection/catalog features** alone, the answers above remain
stable.
