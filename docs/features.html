Stick Picks

Features

Everything Stick Picks does, in one place.

Your humidor, organized

Track every cigar by brand, vitola, and rest time. Mark cigars Owned, Wishlist, or Logged, and capture your own ratings as you build out your tasting journal.

Filter by status, search by name, and see at a glance what's resting and what's earned a place on the wishlist.

A catalog built for discovery

Browse a curated database of premium handmade cigars. Filter by strength, search by brand or name, and explore by flavor, cedar, pepper, cream, baking spice, and dozens more.

Every entry is curated with notes, pairings, and a path back to your humidor.

Match your palate to your humidor

Profile Match asks three questions and matches your palate to catalog entries worth adding to your collection. The Detailed Match goes nine deep for precision matching.

Or skip the questions entirely, point your camera at any cigar band and Identify by Band handles the lookup on the spot.

Your collection at a glance

Your profile keeps a running tally, cigars in your humidor, entries logged, scans completed.

Everything stays private and tied to your account. No social feed, no leaderboards, no nudges to consume, just a clean record of your collection.
