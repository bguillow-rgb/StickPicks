Frequently Asked Questions

What is Stick Picks?

Stick Picks is a free web app for adult cigar collectors. You catalog your humidor, scan bands to identify cigars, track collection value, and log tasting notes, all in one private reference, right in your browser.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Stick Picks runs entirely in your browser at stickpicks.app/app, on desktop or phone. There is nothing to install and no app store required. Add it to your home screen for one-tap access.

Is Stick Picks a retailer?

No. Stick Picks does not sell cigars, distribute tobacco, or promote tobacco consumption. It is a reference, journal, and humidor catalog for collectors.

How old do I have to be to use Stick Picks?

Stick Picks is intended for adults aged 21 and older, matching the United States federal minimum legal age to purchase tobacco. A 21+ age gate is enforced on first launch.

How does the cigar band scanner work?

Snap or upload a clear photo of the cigar band. Our Cigar Concierge reads the brand and line text and matches it against the catalog. If recognition fails, you can retry with a sharper photo, search manually, or suggest a cigar to be added.

Is Stick Picks free?

Yes. The web app is free to use with every feature unlocked, including the band scanner, humidor catalog, collection value tracking, and journaling.

Is my collection data private?

Yes. Your humidor, journal entries, scan history, and ratings are tied to your account and never shared, sold, or used for advertising. See the Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.

Do I need an iPhone or Android phone?

No. Stick Picks works on any device with a modern browser, including desktop, laptop, iPhone, and Android. Native app-store versions are on the roadmap, but the full experience is already available on the web.
