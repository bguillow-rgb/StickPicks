Frequently Asked Questions

What is Stick Picks?

Stick Picks is an iOS journal app for adult cigar collectors. You catalog your humidor, scan bands to identify cigars, track collection value, and log tasting notes, all in one private reference.

Is Stick Picks a retailer?

No. Stick Picks does not sell cigars, distribute tobacco, or promote tobacco consumption. It is a reference, journal, and humidor catalog for collectors.

How old do I have to be to use Stick Picks?

Stick Picks is intended for adults aged 21 and older, matching the United States federal minimum legal age to purchase tobacco. A 21+ age gate is enforced on first launch.

How does the cigar band scanner work?

Point the camera at the cigar band. The scanner reads the brand and line text and matches against the catalog. If recognition fails, you can enhance and retry, search manually, or suggest a cigar to be added.

Is Stick Picks free?

Yes, the core app is free. A Pro subscription unlocks advanced collection-tracking and journaling features. Pro is managed through Apple subscriptions and can be canceled anytime in iOS Settings.

Is my collection data private?

Yes. Your humidor, journal entries, scan history, and ratings are tied to your account and never shared, sold, or used for advertising. See the Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.

Will Stick Picks be available on Android?

Stick Picks launched on iOS first. An Android release is on the roadmap but not yet scheduled.
