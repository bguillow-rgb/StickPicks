## How long do cigars survive a power outage?

[ 200-300 words. The hour-by-hour reality:
- Hours 0-24: passive humidors essentially unaffected. Electronic
  humidors begin RH drift but cigars unaffected.
- Days 1-3: passive humidors at 65-68% RH (slight drift from 70).
  Cedar buffers the swing.
- Days 4-7: passive humidors begin meaningful drift, especially if
  outside ambient is dry. Cigars still smokeable; not yet damaged.
- Day 7+: action required.

First-hand: longest outage you've weathered. ]

## What changes at hour 24?

[ 150-250 words. Electronic humidor specifics: thermoelectric units
stop cooling, internal temperature rises toward room ambient. If
room is below 72°F you have weeks. If room is above 74°F you have
hours before beetle risk activates.

Passive humidor at 24 hours: hygrometer typically reads within 2%
of starting RH. No action needed yet. ]

## What changes after 3 days?

[ 200-300 words. Passive humidor: humidification source partially
depleted; RH drift becoming noticeable but not damaging. Action: add
a 72% Boveda pack if you have one on hand. Do NOT open the lid for
extended periods.

Electronic humidor at 3 days: depends entirely on room temp. Beetle
risk in summer; benign in winter. ]

## How do you stabilize without power?

[ 200-250 words. The toolkit:
- 2-pack of 72% Boveda packs in a sealed gallon freezer bag (offline storage)
- Distilled water (sealed, never used)
- A second hygrometer (calibrated)
- A backup container (Tupperdor) for emergency relocation

Action sequence:
1. Don't open the lid until you have a plan.
2. Check ambient temperature first; if room is climbing past 74°F,
   move the humidor to the coolest interior space.
3. After 72 hours add a fresh 72% Boveda pack.
4. After 5 days consider transferring to a sealed Tupperdor with
   2-3 Boveda packs. ]

## What about electronic humidors?

[ 150-200 words. Wineadors and dedicated coolers (Newair, Whynter)
lose their primary advantage immediately. Cigars inside are still
protected by the cedar lining for 24-48 hours. After that you are
running a passive humidor with worse seal characteristics than a
purpose-built passive box. Plan a Boveda transition by day 2 and a
Tupperdor relocation by day 5 if power is not back. ]

## What should you do before a known outage?

[ 150-200 words. Hurricane / planned outage prep:
1. Top up your humidification source 24 hours before.
2. Place a fresh 72% Boveda inside as a buffer.
3. Pre-cool the room (run AC down to 65°F before the outage hits).
4. Move the humidor to the most thermally stable spot, interior
   closet, ground floor, away from windows.
5. Keep the lid closed. Resist checking.

The single most common mistake: opening the lid every few hours to
"check", each opening costs 1-2 hours of buffer. ]
