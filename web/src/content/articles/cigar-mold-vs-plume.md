## What's the difference between mold and plume?

[ 200-300 words. The four-test diagnostic:

| Test | Plume | Mold |
|---|---|---|
| **Look** | Fine, dusty, even distribution | Fuzzy, raised, often patchy |
| **Color** | White or off-white | White, blue-green, gray, or black |
| **Smell** | None / normal cigar | Musty, basement, damp |
| **Touch** | Brushes off cleanly | Sticks, smears, leaves a stain |

Trust the smell test most. Plume has no smell. Mold smells unmistakably
musty.

First-hand: have you mistaken one for the other? ]

## What does mold look like?

[ 200-300 words. Visual descriptions: white surface fuzz (like fine
hair), blue-green patches (most common in older humidors), gray-black
spots (advanced). Where it shows up first: on the foot (cut end),
under the band, at contact points where two cigars touch.

The "I thought it was plume" trap: distributed fuzziness on a single
cigar surrounded by clean cigars is almost always mold. Plume, when
it appears, tends to appear across multiple aged cigars at once. ]

## What does plume look like?

[ 200-300 words. Visual: a fine even white powder, sometimes called
"crystallization." Distribution: spread across the wrapper, not
clumped. Texture: brushes off with a soft dry brush in seconds.

Plume timing: usually appears on cigars aged 2+ years, more common on
oily wrappers (maduro, oscuro) than dry ones. Its appearance is a
signal that aging is progressing well. ]

## Can you save a moldy cigar?

[ 200-300 words. Surface mold on the wrapper: scrape gently with a
clean blade; if mold did not penetrate (the scraped area shows clean
leaf below), the cigar is salvageable but rest it isolated for 30
days at 65% RH before smoking. Smell again before lighting.

Mold inside the cigar (in the filler or binder): not salvageable.
Cut and discard. Inspect every neighbor in the humidor. Reset humidor
RH to 65% for 30 days as a hygiene cycle. ]

## How do you prevent mold?

[ 150-250 words. The four guardrails:
1. Keep RH below 75%, 70% is the safe target.
2. Calibrate hygrometers every 6 months (salt test).
3. Rotate cigars; do not let the same stick sit in a corner for years.
4. Quarantine new arrivals for 14 days before adding to the main
   humidor. Some cigars arrive with elevated moisture.

Track humidor RH over time so a slow drift is caught before it
becomes a mold event. Soft mention of Stick Picks for per-cigar
acquisition dates and rest tracking. ]

## What about plume, is it desirable?

[ 100-150 words. Yes. Plume on a 5+ year cigar is one of the visual
markers that the leaf has aged well and the oils have crystallized.
Many collectors specifically seek plume on rare and aged sticks.
Smoking experience is unaffected; brush lightly before lighting. ]
