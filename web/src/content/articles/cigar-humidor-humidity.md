  - q: "Is 70 percent humidity too high for cigars?"
    a: "70% is the long-standing default and most cigars store well there. Drier collectors prefer 65-68% for a firmer draw and faster burn; wetter regions push to 72%. Above 75% mold and beetles become real risks."
  - q: "What humidity is too low for cigars?"
    a: "Below 60% is the danger zone. Wrappers crack, oils evaporate, and the burn becomes harsh. Cigars stored under 55% for extended periods rarely recover full flavor even after rehydration."
  - q: "How do I lower humidor humidity?"
    a: "Crack the lid for 30 to 60 minutes to vent. Switch to a lower-RH Boveda pack (65% or 69% instead of 72%). Remove or partially dry your humidification source. Add a small amount of dry Spanish cedar to absorb excess."
  - q: "How accurate are humidor hygrometers?"
    a: "Digital hygrometers are typically accurate to plus or minus 2-3% from the factory. Analog spring hygrometers can drift 5-10% over time. Calibrate with a salt test annually."
